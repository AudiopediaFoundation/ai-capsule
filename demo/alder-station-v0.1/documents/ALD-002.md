# ALD-002 | Alder Learning Station: Equipment and Offline Listening

Record ID: ALD-002
Language: English (en)
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-10-14
Status: SYNTHETIC FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DATA

The Alder Learning Station in this example is entirely fictional. All equipment and operating arrangements below are invented for a retrieval test.

The station has one laptop, one portable speaker, and six pairs of headphones. Twelve example audio stories are stored on the laptop's local storage drive. The station plays these locally stored recordings without an internet connection; no cloud-based audio service is part of this fictional listening workflow.

When the laptop's displayed battery level falls below 30 percent, the fictional volunteer pauses the start of new listening sessions and connects the laptop to a charged power pack. This describes only the invented station's procedure, not safety advice for real equipment.

The equipment record does not identify the station's opening hours or who approves new story recordings. Those subjects are covered by other records.
