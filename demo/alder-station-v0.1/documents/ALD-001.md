# ALD-001 | Alder Learning Station: Access and Schedule

Record ID: ALD-001
Language: English (en)
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-10-14
Status: SYNTHETIC FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DATA

The Alder Learning Station is an entirely fictional mobile listening station invented for AI Capsule testing. It is not a real organization, location, project, or community.

In this example, the station operates in the North Room of a fictional building called Sample Hall. It is open on Tuesdays from 09:00 to 11:00 and on Fridays from 14:00 to 16:00. It does not open on other days.

Visitors in the fictional scenario may listen to the station's audio stories without registering an account or paying a fee. A volunteer hands out headphones at the beginning of a listening session and collects them at the end. The volunteer does not collect visitors' names.

This record describes only the fictional access rules and schedule. It does not describe the station's equipment, the contents of its story collection, or actual visitor numbers.
