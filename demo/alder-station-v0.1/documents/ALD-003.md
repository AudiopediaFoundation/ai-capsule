# ALD-003 | Alder Learning Station: Story Updates and Records

Record ID: ALD-003
Language: English (en)
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-10-14
Status: SYNTHETIC FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DATA

The Alder Learning Station is a fictional test scenario. Its update procedures do not describe real community governance or real authorization.

The example collection initially contains twelve fictional audio stories. A designated station coordinator reviews proposed additions. In the fictional workflow, an operator adds a new story only after the coordinator records approval in a local paper log. The operator then copies the approved recording from a portable storage device onto the station laptop.

The planned update window is the first Monday of each month. Each added story receives a title, revision date, and reviewer name in the fictional station log. This record describes a hypothetical manual process; it does not demonstrate a software-enforced approval boundary.

The fictional record does not contain visitor totals, attendance counts, or usage statistics for any month, including September 2026. No real recordings, story scripts, or personal records are provided in this demonstration pack.
